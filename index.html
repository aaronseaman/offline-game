How to play

Steer the shape

  • Drag left or right to move.
  • Tap the right half to rotate clockwise, the left half to rotate back.
  • Drag down to soft drop. Flick down to drop instantly.
  • Flick up or tap HOLD to keep a shape for later.

Keyboard: ← → move · ↑ or X rotate · Z rotate back · ↓ soft drop · Space drop · C hold · P pause

Match colors, not rows

  • Line up 3 or more blocks of one color in a row or column to clear them. Full rows do nothing.
  • Blocks above fall straight down and can match again. Each cascade step multiplies your points.
  • Speed rises each level and a sixth color joins at level 5. If a new shape can't enter at the top, the game ends.

Swap to finish a match

  • When a shape lands and you have a swap banked, glowing blocks show every swap that makes a match.
  • Tap a block, then a neighbor. Or drag it onto the neighbor. Tap empty space to skip.
  • Each landing and each cleared block fills the gold meter. A full meter is one swap, and you can bank 3. Chains of 3 and 5 give a swap outright.

Special blocks

  • 4 in a line → Line blaster. When matched it clears its whole row or column, along its stripes.
  • L or T shape → Bomb. Clears a 3×3 area.
  • 5 in a line → Prism. Clears every block of its color. Swap it with any block to wipe out that block's color instead.

Swap two specials into each other for a bigger blast: crosses, 5×5 explosions, or whole colors at once.
